Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Score Tone handles Google account information, imported scores, and data saved in your browser. Google Drive is optional; you can import local scores without signing in. Account libraries organize local data but are not an authentication lock or app-level encrypted storage.

Google API Services & User Data

Score Tone integrates with Google API Services to open PDF, MusicXML, and compressed MusicXML (MXL) scores. The deployment uses one shared OAuth project; each user consents with their own Google account. Score Tone's use of information received from Google APIs is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Score Tone (Google Drive & Picker Integration)

Drive Access: Score Tone requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file for files authorized for this app, normally through your selection in Google Picker. The native "Previously authorized scores" list is limited by this scope; it is not a list of your entire Drive. Google describes this scope as permitting modification of authorized files, but Score Tone currently uses it to read metadata and download scores, not upload edits, delete Drive originals, or change sharing permissions. Metadata may include file ID, name, size, modification time, and thumbnail URL.

Account Information: Score Tone also requests openid, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email, and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile. During explicit sign-in, it verifies the returned account by calling Google's userinfo endpoint with the access token. The returned stable account identifier (sub) selects the account's local library; available name, given name, email, and profile picture URL support account display. A typed email or saved login hint is not proof of account identity.

Links and Public Files: Pasting or sharing a score link does not grant access. Private files may require both access from the owner and selection through Google Picker. Downloads may also try public Google endpoints, so some publicly accessible files can open without Picker authorization or a token. Public downloads remain subject to Google's restrictions and browser/network behavior. Links share file references and may include names, page positions, or loop details; they do not upload local file content or transfer permission.

Data Usage and Transfers: Score Tone uses Google user data to identify the selected account, display account information, list authorized scores, and download, display, play supported scores, and retain local copies as requested. It does not use this data for advertising, marketing, or behavioral profiling, or sell it. These operations run in your browser and communicate directly with Google for authentication, userinfo, Picker, file requests, and Google-hosted profile/thumbnail images. There is no Score Tone backend receiving score content or maintaining a cloud library, managed sharing service, or cross-device synchronization.

Offline Data Retention: Local imports and scores imported through the Drive library dialog save metadata and file blobs in the currently selected library's IndexedDB database. Account libraries are separate databases keyed by Google account identifier and include files, bookmarks, offline blobs, annotations, and custom presets. A newly opened shared link may remain an in-memory preview until saved. The original ScoreToneDatabase remains the "Device library," including older saved scores; connecting an account does not automatically reassign or migrate it. Local imports while an account library is selected save there, not automatically in Device library, and are not uploaded to Google Drive.

Saved Profile and Login Hint: The selected account's identifier and available display profile are saved in your browser's localStorage, along with a login hint for a later explicit reconnect. This remembers the selected offline library after reload, even when offline or without a live Google session. The hint may be sent to Google when you explicitly reconnect. Restoring this profile is not fresh identity verification. Choosing another Google account requires an explicit online flow and verification through Google's userinfo endpoint.

OAuth Tokens: Access tokens and expiry timestamps are held only in JavaScript memory, not in localStorage, sessionStorage, or IndexedDB. Reloading the page loses the token. Legacy localStorage token and expiry keys are removed at startup and disconnect. Score Tone stores no refresh token and performs no silent or background authentication. Reconnect explicitly when online access is needed; saved offline copies do not require a live token. Preloading Google's sign-in script is not an authentication request.

Local Privacy Limits: Requests to Google APIs use HTTPS, but Score Tone does not provide app-level encryption or an authentication lock for browser-stored scores. Someone using the same OS user or browser profile can access or alter browser storage, including retained account libraries. Separate OS/browser profiles are appropriate for shared devices. Offline storage can be lost through browser eviction, private browsing, or clearing site data; keep original scores elsewhere.

General Data Handling

  • No Score Tone Account Required: Local PDF, MusicXML, and MXL import requires no registration. Connecting Google is optional and enables selected-file import and an account-specific local library. If Google services are unavailable, use device-file import; disabling Brave Shields is not required to use Score Tone.
  • Browser Storage: Libraries are stored in IndexedDB; the selected profile, login hint, and general preferences use localStorage. General display settings, theme, and library view preference remain device-wide, unlike account-scoped saved custom presets. The service worker caches app assets for offline use. Browser storage is not a cloud backup.
  • No Analytics/Tracking: We do not track your usage habits or sell data to advertising networks.

Third-Party Terms & Revocation

Google services are subject to Google's Privacy Policy. The app also loads resources from external providers; this privacy page uses Google Fonts and the Tailwind CSS CDN. Requests to these services and the hosting provider can expose standard connection information such as your IP address and browser information under those providers' policies.

Disconnect: "Disconnect · use device library" clears the in-memory token and saved profile/login hint, hides that account's library, and returns to Device library. It does not delete account copies, bookmarks, annotations, or custom presets, and it does not revoke Google's permission grant. Reconnecting the same verified account shows its retained library again.

Revoke: To revoke Google's grant, remove Score Tone from Google account permissions. Revocation does not erase existing offline copies or local profile data; disconnect and delete local data separately as needed.

Delete Local Scores: Use the trash action in the selected library to delete a score's metadata, bookmarks, blob, and annotations from that library. Other account libraries and Google Drive originals are unaffected. Removing only an offline copy retains metadata; remove saved custom presets separately. To delete a hidden account's scores through the app, reconnect that account first.

Delete All Local App Data: Close other Score Tone tabs and clear this site's browser data, including IndexedDB, localStorage, and caches, to remove all Device and account libraries, saved profiles, preferences, and offline app assets in that browser profile. Clearing site data does not delete Drive originals or revoke Google's grant. Account copies otherwise remain until you remove them or the browser clears them; disconnect is not secure erasure.
